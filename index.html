FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Clear answers regarding our turnkey execution process, material authenticity, warranty terms, and project timelines.

We execute projects through parallel milestone scheduling. Once 3D layouts and the itemized BOQ are finalized, your custom modular kitchen and wardrobes are precision-manufactured off-site. Simultaneously, our on-site team executes false ceiling framing, electrical conduits, plumbing, and wall prep. By Day 38–42, cabinetry is assembled and deep cleaning completed for flawless Day 45 handover.

We exclusively specify certified, 100% authentic brands: Blum Austria and Hettich Germany for soft-close hinges and Tandembox drawer runners; Saint-Gobain Gyproc for acoustic false ceiling channels and boards; Asian Paints Royale & PU for wall textures; and Kohler / Jaquar Artize for luxury sanitary fittings.

Zero hidden costs. Our transparent Bill of Quantities (BOQ) details every square foot, laminate grade, hardware model, and finish specification in advance. Unless you request an optional scope change during site work, the agreed contract price remains strictly unchanged.

Yes! We offer a private design consultation where our team studies your architectural floor plan, understands your style preferences (Modern, Contemporary, or Luxury Minimalist), and provides a comprehensive initial budget roadmap.
