Privacy Policy

At KMSJ Interiors, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us while enquiring about residential interiors, modular kitchens, false ceilings, renovation projects, architectural consulting, or site evaluations.

We may collect details such as your name, phone number, email address, project location, service interest, home requirements, and message content when you contact us through our website, WhatsApp, email, or direct consultation request forms.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

Data Protection

We treat personal information as confidential and take reasonable steps to protect it from unauthorised access, misuse, and disclosure. Information is stored securely and only retained as long as necessary for the stated purpose or as required by law.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share details only with internal teams or trusted service providers when required to fulfil your enquiry, provide a quotation, or deliver project support.
